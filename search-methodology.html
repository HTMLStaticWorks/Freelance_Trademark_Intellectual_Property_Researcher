Our Methodology

A rigorous, multi-layered approach to trademark clearance.

1

Lexical Parsing & Queries

We break down the proposed mark into its core lexical components, generating hundreds of wildcard queries targeting phonetic equivalents, purposeful misspellings, and translated equivalents.

2

Automated Data Scraping

Our algorithms scan the USPTO TESS database, filtering millions of records down to a highly relevant subset based on International Classes and Coordinated Classes.

3

Manual Human Verification

Trained IP analysts review the algorithmic output to assess nuanced likelihood of confusion, checking for "merely descriptive" vulnerabilities or geographic significance.

Our Risk Assessment Framework

Targeted Precision

Eliminating false positives by filtering results strictly through the lens of established TTAB precedents.

Vulnerability Scoring

Quantifying the exact likelihood of receiving a likelihood of confusion or descriptiveness refusal.

Actionable Briefs

Delivering not just data, but structured arguments that your legal team can immediately use.
