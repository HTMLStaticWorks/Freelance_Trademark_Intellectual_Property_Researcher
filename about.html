Notable Case Studies

Tech Startup Rebranding

A Silicon Valley startup was preparing a $50M Series B announcement. Our clearance search uncovered a dormant but actively renewed registration in Germany that matched their proposed name identically in Class 09. We provided immediate guidance to slightly alter the mark, saving them from an inevitable opposition and forced rebrand post-launch.

Saved: $2.5M in potential litigation

Pharmaceutical Clearance

Prior to FDA approval, a pharmaceutical giant needed clearance for a new drug name. Using our advanced phonetic algorithms, we identified a highly similar sounding mark in Class 05 (Dietary Supplements). We delivered a comprehensive TTAB litigation history of the opposing party, allowing our client's counsel to proactively negotiate a coexistence agreement.

Result: Successful Registration
